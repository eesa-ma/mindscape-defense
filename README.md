# Mindscape Defense

## Overview

Mindscape Defense is an educational survival game designed to raise awareness about loneliness and mental well-being. The game represents loneliness as a combination of emotional, social, and psychological challenges rather than simply a lack of social interaction.

Players must survive within a symbolic "Mindscape" where challenges such as social rejection, academic pressure, burnout, isolation, and negative thoughts emerge over time. By choosing appropriate coping strategies, players can protect their emotional well-being, maintain social connection, and prevent loneliness from taking over.

The game combines engaging gameplay mechanics with educational objectives, helping players understand the various causes of loneliness and the importance of healthy coping mechanisms.

---

## Objective

The primary objective is to survive for as long as possible while maintaining the Connection Meter and preserving lives.

Players must respond to different challenges before they negatively impact their emotional state.

### Win Condition

* Survive until the game timer ends.
* Maintain a positive Connection Meter.

### Lose Condition

* Connection Meter reaches zero and all lives are exhausted.

---

## Gameplay

### Challenge System

Throughout the game, emotional and social challenges appear within the Mindscape.

Examples include:

* Academic Pressure
* Social Rejection
* Burnout
* Isolation
* Negative Thoughts

Each challenge requires an appropriate coping strategy.

For example:

| Challenge         | Effective Response  |
| ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Academic Pressure | Time Management     |
| Burnout           | Rest                |
| Isolation         | Reach Out           |
| Negative Thoughts | Positive Reflection |
| Social Rejection  | Seek Support        |

Correct decisions increase score and improve emotional well-being, while incorrect decisions reduce connection and increase difficulty.

---

### Connection Meter

The Connection Meter represents the player's emotional and social well-being.

* Starts at 100%.
* Decreases when challenges are ignored or handled incorrectly.
* Can be restored through successful responses.

As the Connection Meter decreases, the visual fog within the Mindscape becomes denser, symbolizing increasing loneliness.

---

### Lives System

Players begin with three lives.

Lives are lost when:

* Connection reaches zero.
* Multiple challenges are mishandled.

The game ends when all lives are lost.

---

### Scoring System

Players earn points by:

* Correctly resolving challenges.
* Maintaining high connection levels.
* Creating response streaks.
* Surviving longer durations.

---

### Difficulty Progression

As gameplay progresses:

* Challenges appear more frequently.
* Decision time decreases.
* Multiple challenges may appear simultaneously.
* Higher penalties are applied for incorrect decisions.

This ensures increasing difficulty and engagement.

---

## Educational Purpose

Mindscape Defense aims to educate players about the multifaceted nature of loneliness.

The game highlights that loneliness can arise from:

* Social isolation
* Academic stress
* Burnout
* Rejection
* Negative self-perception
* Family and personal conflicts

By interacting with these scenarios, players learn that different situations require different coping strategies and that seeking support is an important part of maintaining emotional well-being.
